India, officially the Republic of India, is a country in South Asia. It is the seventh-largest country by area; the most populous country since 2023; and, since its independence in 1947, the world's most populous democracy.

India's "best friends" or closest strategic partners are generally identified as Russia, Israel, Japan, France, and Bhutan due to long-term trust, defense cooperation, and diplomatic support. These nations are characterized by consistent, reliable partnerships, often described as "time-tested" or "strategic," rather than solely based on transactional interests

India is currently grappling with a surge of influenza infections, predominantly caused by a virulent strain known as H3N2. Given how quickly this viral outbreak spreads, people must recognise its prevalence rate and take the appropriate safety measures.
